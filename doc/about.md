# About Küdaw

![Küdaw](/nifi-monitoring-splunk/assets/images/kudaw.png){ width="220" }

**Nifi Monitoring** and **Nifi Monitoring TA** are developed and maintained
by [Küdaw](https://www.kudaw.com), specialists in data and AI.

Küdaw was founded in Chile in 2010 to bring data and artificial intelligence
technologies within reach of companies in the Americas and Europe. Today it
works from Mexico, the United States, Canada, Spain, Switzerland, Chile, Peru
and Colombia, and is a strategic partner of Splunk, Cloudera, Microsoft, Qlik
and Google Cloud, among others.

## Why these apps exist

Küdaw builds and runs Splunk and data platforms for its clients, and Apache
NiFi moves the data in many of them. Watching NiFi meant opening each
instance's UI, one at a time; these apps bring that into Splunk, next to
everything else.

They are free and open source, under the MIT license, and published on
Splunkbase: [Nifi Monitoring](https://splunkbase.splunk.com/app/6125) and
[Nifi Monitoring TA](https://splunkbase.splunk.com/app/6124). Contributions
are welcome — see [Contribute](contributing.md).

## What Küdaw does

- **Cybersecurity and observability with Splunk**: SIEM, SOAR and a 24/7
  SOC.
- **Data platforms**: data lakes, warehouses and ETL pipelines — NiFi
  among them.
- **Advanced analytics**: dashboards, BI and predictive models.
- **AI and automation**: AI agents, chatbots and LLMs.
- **Compliance**: automating data-subject rights and consent management.
